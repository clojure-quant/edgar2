Counts from `data/companyfacts.zip` published 2026-10-17. The zip has 20,472 CIK files; 18,001 of them report at least one taxonomy. `demo.facts-taxonomies` counts a filer once for each key under `facts`. Companyfacts keeps standard-taxonomy tags and leaves custom extension tags out.

A closed-end fund is a filer with `:cef`. An ADR has no taxonomy of its own. ADR facts are under `:us-gaap` or `:ifrs-full`.

| :taxonomy | :number-of-filers | notes |
|---|---:|---|
| :dei | 16587 | Document and Entity Information, maintained by the SEC at xbrl.sec.gov/dei. Cover-page facts required on almost every EDGAR XBRL submission: registrant name, central index key, ticker, exchange, document type, fiscal period, and shares outstanding. The EDGAR XBRL Guide (October 2026) lists `dei` as a required entry point for the general instance type. |
| :us-gaap | 16571 | GAAP Financial Reporting Taxonomy, published by FASB. Namespace prefix `us-gaap` (xbrl.fasb.org/us-gaap). Primary financial statements and note disclosures for filers that report under US GAAP, including Forms 10-K and 10-Q. EDGAR accepts the annual versions FASB publishes and that the SEC lists as standard taxonomies. |
| :ffd | 3302 | Filing Fee Disclosure, an SEC taxonomy. Tags the Inline XBRL exhibit EX-FILING FEES, which is a separate instance from the financial statements and must not be mixed into them. The tables are the submission, the fees summary, fees by offering, fees by offset, and fees by combined prospectus. EDGAR has accepted FFD since January 2024. |
| :srt | 2252 | SEC Reporting Taxonomy, published by FASB beside US GAAP. Namespace fasb.org/srt. Elements that are not GAAP measurement but that GAAP filers use for SEC requirements: Regulation S-X financial schedules, guarantor condensed consolidating information, oil- and gas-producing activities, bank statistical disclosures, broker-dealer capital, and dimensions in common use. |
| :ecd | 1993 | Executive Compensation Disclosure, an SEC taxonomy (xbrl.sec.gov/ecd). Used on proxy and information statements. Covers Regulation S-K Item 402(v) pay versus performance (compensation actually paid, company and peer total shareholder return, net income) and insider-trading disclosures. Entry point `ecd-sub` is the one for submissions with limited tagging, such as Schedule 14A. |
| :invest | 1674 | Legacy SEC Investments taxonomy, last published as invest-2013-01-31. It held investment-holding concepts and DerivativeNotionalAmount, which the US GAAP taxonomy used as a calculation total. EDGAR stopped accepting the 2012 and 2013 INVEST taxonomies on 9 March 2020. Companyfacts still stores the old facts. A filer with `:invest` is not a fund. |
| :ifrs-full | 972 | Full IFRS Accounting Taxonomy entry point, published by the IFRS Foundation. Prefix `ifrs-full`. Tags financial statements prepared under IFRS Accounting Standards, as used by foreign private issuers on Forms 20-F and 6-K. This is the full-IFRS entry point, not the IFRS for SMEs taxonomy. EDGAR accepted the 2025 IFRS taxonomy in Release 25.2 on 16 June 2025. |
| :cef | 354 | Closed-End Fund, an SEC taxonomy for registered closed-end funds and business development companies that file Form N-2. Tags the cover, fee table, senior securities, effects of leverage, share price, and premium or discount to net asset value. A positive LatestPremiumDiscountToNavPercent is a premium; a negative value is a discount. Companyfacts also shows these tags on Forms N-CSR and N-CSRS. |
| :spac | 113 | Special Purpose Acquisition Company, an SEC taxonomy. Exhibit 98 (instance type EX98.SPAC) on registration statements, proxies, and tender offers. Tags SPAC disclosures such as sponsor compensation, with members for founder shares, public units, common shares, and warrants. The de-SPAC entry point is `spac-despac`. |
| :vip | 88 | Variable Insurance Product, an SEC taxonomy for variable annuity and variable life contracts. The 2026 release notes point at Form N-4 Item 4 and Form N-6 Items 4(a) and 4(b) for portfolio-company expense percentages, split into amounts before and after waivers. Filers in this zip include insurance-company separate accounts. |
| :rxp | 38 | Resource Extraction Payments, an SEC taxonomy for Form SD Exhibit 2.01. Resource extraction issuers tag payments to governments by payment type, government, and project, under Dodd-Frank Section 1504. AlternativeReportingRegime says whether the issuer uses the EU, UK, Norway, or Canada ESTMA regime, or none of them. |
| :oef | 4 | Open-End Fund, an SEC taxonomy for mutual funds and exchange-traded funds. Entry point `oef-rr` tags the Form N-1A prospectus risk/return summary. Entry point `oef-sr` tags Form N-CSR shareholder reports, including holdings and performance. Four filers in this zip have it, among them MFS Municipal Series Trust. |
| :us-gaap-ebp | 2 | Employee Benefit Plan entry point inside the FASB GAAP taxonomy. Prefix `us-gaap-ebp`, in relationship groups numbered from 9611. Form 11-K annual reports of employee stock purchase, savings, and similar plans. The tags are from the plan's perspective (plan assets, employer and participant contributions), not the employer's Form 10-K. |
| :fnd | 1 | Fund, an SEC taxonomy for the definition of terms used in a fund's name. Entry points are `fnd-oef` (Form N-1A), `fnd-cef` (Form N-2), and `fnd-uit` (Forms N-8B-2 and S-6, unit investment trusts). It is used with the open-end and closed-end taxonomies, not instead of them. The only filer in this zip is Floor & Decor Holdings, Inc., and its eight facts are 10-Q operating items (leases, gift cards) with blank labels, not fund-name terms. |

Sources: EDGAR XBRL Guide (October 2026); SEC standard-taxonomy list and Release 26.1 (16 March 2026); SEC taxonomy release notes for 2012, 2013, 2020, and 2026; CEF, OEF, ECD, and FND taxonomy guides on xbrl.sec.gov; FASB GAAP, SRT, and employee-benefit-plan technical guides (2026); IFRS Foundation, IFRS Accounting Taxonomy.
